How the industry average is calculated: The baseline (0.30 Wh per simple prompt) is derived from the only two publicly disclosed per-prompt figures: Google's comprehensive methodology for Gemini (0.24 Wh, Aug 2025) and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's disclosure for ChatGPT (0.34 Wh, Jun 2025). No model-specific breakdown is shown — the goal is awareness of AI's collective footprint, not comparison between products.

Complexity multipliers: Simple = baseline (0.30 Wh). Moderate = 3× (0.90 Wh). Complex = 10× (3.0 Wh). Based on research showing 10–50× variation by query depth (Jegham et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025).

CO₂ estimated using IEA 2025 global average grid intensity (0.43 gCO₂/kWh). Water estimated using industry midpoint cooling assumptions. Both figures vary significantly by data center location and energy source.
